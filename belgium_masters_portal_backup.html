How Belgium fees work

  • Wallonia/Brussels (UCLouvain, ULiège, UNamur, ULB): full fee is €1,194 for 2026-27 (indexed yearly). Non-EU students pay an extra €4,175 unless their nationality is on the ARES list. Pakistan was on the 2025-26 list; confirm it is in the 2026-27 annexes before you rely on it. Income-based rates of €835, €374 or €0 exist, with the application due by 31 October each year.
  • Flanders: non-EU tuition is rising (University of Antwerp master’s: €5,800 → €7,800 in 2026-27). Treat Flemish programs as scholarship-dependent.
  • Master Mind: €10,225/yr plus tuition waiver (about €136–140 admin fee). You cannot apply directly: the university nominates you, only about 30 awards exist, and host deadlines run January–April. The 2027-28 call is under review.
  • Erasmus Mundus: tuition waiver plus about €1,400/month. BDMA’s EU funding ended in October 2026; its successor at ULB is DEAI. You cannot combine Erasmus Mundus with Master Mind.
  • Proof of means: the ~€10,000 figure is living money, not tuition. You can show it with a Belgian bank account, a scholarship letter, or an Annex 32 sponsor.
